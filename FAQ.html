Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about using the StudentHub platform.

General Questions

1. What is StudentHub?

StudentHub is a student-focused platform that provides access to academic information, college events and student services.

2. Who can use StudentHub?

Students and authorized college staff can use the platform's available services.

Account Questions

3. How can I create an account?

Visit the Register page, enter the required information and submit the registration form.

4. How can I log in to StudentHub?

Open the Login page and enter your registered email address and password.

5. Where can I view my profile?

Visit the Profile page to view your personal and academic information.

Events and Activities

6. Where can I find college events?

Visit the Events page to explore technical, cultural and academic activities.

7. How can I get event information?

Check the Events page or contact the StudentHub support team for more information.

Support Questions

8. How can I contact StudentHub?

Visit the Contact page to find support information and send an enquiry.

9. How can I share my suggestions?

Visit the Feedback page and submit your suggestions or comments.

Still Have Questions?

If you cannot find the answer to your question, contact our support team.
